How can we help?

Welcome to the Daily Motif support page. Whether you have a question about the app, need help with your subscription, or have encountered a problem, you'll find some useful information below.

Frequently asked questions

How do I listen to a piece?

Open Daily Motif and press the Home Tab.

How do I access the archive?

You can browse previously featured pieces using the archive feature, subject to your subscription.

How do I manage my subscription?

Your subscription is managed through your Apple App Store or Google Play account, depending on where you subscribed. Open your device's subscription settings to view, change, or cancel your subscription.

The app isn't working correctly. What should I do?

Try closing and reopening the app and checking that you have an internet connection. If the problem persists, contact us using the details below.
