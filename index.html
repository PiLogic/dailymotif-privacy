Privacy Policy

Daily Motif · Last updated: 11 October 2026

1. Introduction

DailyMotif is a music discovery application that introduces users to a piece of music each day. This Privacy Policy explains how DailyMotif handles information when you use the application.

DailyMotif does not require users to create an account and does not operate its own system for collecting or storing personal information about users.

2. Information We Collect

DailyMotif does not require registration, and we do not directly collect or maintain personal profiles, names, email addresses, or other personal information about users.

Certain application preferences and features, such as themes, listening streaks and notification settings, may be stored locally on your device.

The application retrieves music selections and descriptions from an online data source. These requests provide the music content needed for the application to function.

3. Third-Party Services

DailyMotif uses third-party services to provide certain features. These services may process information in accordance with their own privacy policies.

YouTube

DailyMotif displays music videos using YouTube’s embedded player. Users do not need to sign in to YouTube to use this feature.

When you view an embedded video, YouTube may collect information about your interaction with its services, subject to Google’s applicable privacy policies and settings.

For further information, please consult Google’s Privacy Policy .

RevenueCat

DailyMotif uses RevenueCat to manage premium subscriptions and check subscription entitlements.

RevenueCat and the relevant app store may process information associated with purchases and subscriptions to provide these services. This may include transaction information and technical identifiers.

For further information, please consult RevenueCat’s Privacy Policy .

Apple App Store and Google Play

If you purchase a subscription, Apple or Google may process payment and transaction information in accordance with their respective privacy policies and terms.

DailyMotif does not directly receive or store your complete payment card details.

4. Notifications

DailyMotif can schedule notifications locally on your device.

Notification preferences and scheduled notification details are managed by the application on your device. DailyMotif does not require a remote notification server to deliver these scheduled notifications.

You can change your notification preferences or disable notifications through the application or your device settings.

5. Data Storage and Security

DailyMotif does not maintain its own user database or personal user accounts.

Some application preferences may be stored locally on your device. Information processed by third-party services is subject to their respective privacy policies and practices.

We take reasonable care in developing and maintaining the application, but no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Children’s Privacy

DailyMotif does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

The application does not require users to provide personal information or create an account. Third-party services integrated into the application may nevertheless process information in accordance with their own policies and applicable requirements.

7. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as DailyMotif develops or introduces new features. Any revised policy will be published at the same public address, with the updated date shown at the top.
